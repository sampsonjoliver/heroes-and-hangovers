01 · Do Heroic Shit

Do something worthy of a class.

Something impressive. Reckless. Charming. Ridiculous. Clever. Generous. Chaotic. If it feels worthy of an adventurer, it can become a Heroic Feat.

You can call your own Feat. Someone else can spot one after it happens. The group decides whether it counts.

02 · Gain a Rank

The day builds your character.

Each class has five ranks. Heroic Feats gain you levels and unlock new abilities.

You can multiclass. Your total level is the number of ranks you earn across your classes. The things you actually do determine the character you become.

Highest level at the end of the night wins. The prize is eternal glory, which is to say probably nothing.

A Heroic Feat earns a rank in the class that best matches what you actually did.

03 · Make an Attack

Every Heroic Feat earns an Attack.

Choose another player and roll a d20. Your Attack inflicts Damage. As your character develops, abilities can modify Attacks and Damage—sometimes dramatically.

Natural 1: your Attack backfires onto you.
2–19: your Attack hits.
Natural 20: critical hit — double Damage.

1 Damage = 1 drink or 1 punishment
1 drink = 1 sip

THE BUCK

Quest Giver
The Buck's Unique Trait

Quest Giver

The Buck may declare a Quest: a specific, harmless objective for his increasingly dysfunctional adventuring party. Any player may attempt it.

When the Quest is completed, the group decides which class best represents how it was accomplished. The player responsible earns a Heroic Feat in that class as normal.
